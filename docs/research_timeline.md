This is a chronological map of the Decision-OS / Value Dynamics publications and selected repository anchors. For a first reading, use the [interest-based paths](../README.md#start-here-gateway) or [current series index](../README.md#series-index-zenodo); the timeline is not required reading.

## Source and date convention

Rechecked **2026-09-13** against the publisher-supplied Zenodo metadata registered at DataCite, rather than treating this file's previous “confirmed” labels as verification. Each row links to its exact DOI registration. All listed version DOI records returned `state: findable`. Direct Zenodo pages/API/downloads timed out in this environment; archival file availability and local/deposit byte identity were not verified.

Titles are the registered titles. Dates below use the first `Issued` entry; **†** marks registrations that also retain a different `Issued` date, listed after the table. A PDF header/build date, a repository commit date, and a publication date are separate observations. “Published” means public deposition, not peer review or empirical validation. Full Paper versus Note describes the manuscript's role; Preprint / Working paper / Text describes the registered resource type.

- **V10:** the earlier Note `19871495` (`v1.0`) and Full Paper `20371623` (`v2.0`) are both listed by the [concept 19871494 registration](https://api.datacite.org/dois/10.5281/zenodo.19871494). The full paper's local `_v1` filenames do not determine its deposited version.
- **V11:** Full Paper `20301056` (`v2.0`) explicitly declares `IsNewVersionOf` Note `19872064` (`v1.0`). Both belong to concept `19872063`; the Note must not be cited as though it were the full paper.

These are relationships within particular works. Series order is not blanket supersession, and a concept DOI does not identify the entire Decision-OS series.
