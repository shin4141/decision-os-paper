## Citation and evidence boundary

Use the [version DOI index](../README.md#series-index-zenodo) and [publication timeline](../docs/research_timeline.md). Filenames, series numbers, and deposited version labels are different identifiers; V6's local `v2` file, for example, has deposited version `V4`.

Zenodo DOIs identify archival papers. This repository routes readers to those papers and preserves local source states; software repositories control their own implementation and schemas. Historical SSOT labels apply to their named edition or commit only. A local PDF is not claimed byte-identical to a deposit unless an actual comparison establishes that identity.

V10/V11 short Notes remain available alongside their later full papers. V13 and V14 remain research notes, and publication does not remove their draft or prototype boundaries. Public availability is not evidence of peer review or general performance. See [verification and unresolved items](../docs/gateway_update_2026-09-13.md).
